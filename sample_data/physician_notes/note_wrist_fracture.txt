Chief Complaint: Right wrist pain after fall

HPI: 29-year-old female presents after a fall onto an outstretched hand
while running. Immediate pain and swelling of the right wrist.

Exam: Visible deformity and swelling of the right wrist. Tenderness over
the distal radius. Neurovascularly intact distally.

Assessment: Fracture of wrist, right side, initial encounter.

Plan: Wrist x-ray obtained confirming distal radius fracture. Closed
treatment of distal radial fracture performed with splinting. Follow up
with orthopedics in 1 week.
