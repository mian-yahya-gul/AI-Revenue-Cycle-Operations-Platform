Cigna Healthcare — Medical Necessity and Coverage Guidelines
Policy Number: CIG-MN-2024-22
Effective Date: May 1, 2024

MEDICAL NECESSITY DEFINITION
Cigna defines a medically necessary service as one that is required to
diagnose or treat an illness, injury, disease, or its symptoms, is in
accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice, and
is not solely for the convenience of the patient or provider.

CODING ACCURACY REQUIREMENTS
Claims must reflect the most specific ICD-10 code supported by clinical
documentation. Unspecified diagnosis codes (e.g., codes ending in
'.9' where a more specific code exists) may trigger additional review
and are a common cause of claim delay. CPT codes must accurately reflect
the complexity and content of the service rendered; upcoding or
downcoding relative to the documented note is grounds for claim denial
and potential audit.

MISSING PHYSICIAN NOTES
Cigna requires that a signed physician note or operative report be
available to support every billed service. Claims for which supporting
documentation cannot be produced upon request are subject to denial and
potential recoupment of previously paid amounts under reason code
CO-B7.

TIMELY FILING
Claims must be submitted within 120 days of the date of service.
Claims submitted after this window will be denied under reason code
CO-29 (timely filing limit expired) regardless of medical necessity.
