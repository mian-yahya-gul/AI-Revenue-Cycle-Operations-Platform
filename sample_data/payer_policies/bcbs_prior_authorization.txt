Blue Cross Blue Shield — Prior Authorization Requirements
Policy Number: BCBS-PA-2024-05
Effective Date: February 15, 2024

SERVICES REQUIRING PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) requires prior authorization for:
1. Inpatient hospital admissions (non-emergent).
2. Advanced imaging services (MRI, CT, PET, nuclear medicine studies).
3. Outpatient surgical procedures performed in an ambulatory surgery
   center with an allowed amount exceeding $2,000.
4. Home infusion therapy and specialty pharmaceuticals.
5. Applied behavior analysis (ABA) therapy beyond the initial evaluation.

DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
Requests must include a completed prior authorization form, clinical
notes from the ordering or treating physician, relevant diagnostic
results, and accurate ICD-10 and CPT/HCPCS codes. BCBS reviews requests
within 5 business days for standard requests and 72 hours for expedited
(urgent) requests.

DENIALS FOR MISSING AUTHORIZATION
Claims for services requiring prior authorization that are submitted
without an approval on file are denied under reason code CO-197.
Network providers may not bill the member for the denied amount
(no balance billing) except in cases where the member was notified in
advance in writing and agreed to be financially responsible.

APPEAL RIGHTS
Providers may file a first-level appeal within 180 days of the denial
notice. The appeal must include all supporting clinical documentation
and a written statement addressing the specific basis for the original
denial.
