Cigna Healthcare — Prior Authorization and Utilization Management Policy
Policy Number: CIG-UM-2024-09
Effective Date: January 15, 2024

SERVICES REQUIRING PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Cigna requires prior authorization for:
1. All elective inpatient admissions and skilled nursing facility
   admissions.
2. Outpatient advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET).
3. Spinal surgery, joint replacement surgery, and bariatric surgery.
4. Specialty infusion drugs administered outside the home setting.
5. Durable medical equipment rentals or purchases exceeding $750.

DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS
All prior authorization requests must include the treating physician's
clinical rationale, relevant diagnostic imaging or lab results, a
description of conservative treatments already attempted (where
applicable), and the specific ICD-10 and CPT/HCPCS codes for the
requested service. Incomplete requests will be returned to the
requesting provider without a determination.

CLAIMS WITHOUT PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Services rendered without required prior authorization will be denied
under reason code CO-197. Cigna does not guarantee retrospective
authorization; requests for retrospective review must be submitted
within 90 days of the date of service and must include a clear
explanation of any extenuating circumstances (e.g., emergent
conversion from an outpatient to inpatient procedure).

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION VALIDITY
An approved prior authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of
approval unless otherwise specified on the authorization letter. Claims
submitted after the authorization has expired will be treated as if no
authorization was obtained.

APPEALS
Cigna allows two levels of internal appeal. Level 1 appeals must be
submitted within 180 days of the denial and are reviewed by a clinician
who was not involved in the original determination. Level 2 appeals
require new clinical information not previously considered.
