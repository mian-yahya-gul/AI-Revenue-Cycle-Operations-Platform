Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin — General Coverage Criteria
Policy Number: AET-CPB-2024-15
Effective Date: April 1, 2024

COVERAGE DETERMINATION STANDARD
Aetna considers a service medically necessary when it is required to
identify or treat a member's illness or injury and meets accepted
standards of medicine. Services that are primarily for the convenience
of the member or provider, or that are duplicative of a previously
performed and paid service, are not covered.

DUPLICATE CLAIM POLICY
A claim is considered a duplicate when the same procedure code, same
date of service, same rendering provider, and same patient appear on a
previously adjudicated claim. Duplicate claims are denied under reason
code CO-18 unless the second claim includes modifier -76 (repeat
procedure by same physician) or modifier -77 (repeat procedure by
another physician) with supporting clinical documentation explaining
the medical necessity of the repeat service.

REQUIRED CLAIM ELEMENTS
Every submitted claim must contain complete patient eligibility
information, an accurate ICD-10 diagnosis code that is an exact match
to the documented condition in the medical record, an appropriate CPT
or HCPCS code, the correct place-of-service code, and rendering
provider NPI. Claims with mismatched or unsupported diagnosis-to-
procedure code combinations are subject to automated coding edits and
may be denied under reason code CO-11 (diagnosis inconsistent with
procedure).

BEHAVIORAL HEALTH SERVICES
Outpatient behavioral health visits do not require precertification for
the first 8 visits per calendar year. Visits beyond this threshold
require submission of a treatment plan and precertification approval.
