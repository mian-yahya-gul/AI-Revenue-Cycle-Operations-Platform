UnitedHealthcare Commercial Plan — Prior Authorization Policy
Policy Number: UHC-PA-2024-11
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

SCOPE
This policy applies to all UnitedHealthcare Commercial PPO and HMO plans
for outpatient and inpatient procedures billed under CPT codes in the
surgical, advanced imaging, and durable medical equipment categories.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED SERVICES
The following categories of service require prior authorization before
the service is rendered. Claims submitted without an approved
authorization number on file will be denied.

1. Advanced imaging: MRI, MRA, CT, and PET scans (CPT 70000–79999 range)
   require prior authorization except when performed in an emergency
   department setting with a documented emergency diagnosis.
2. Inpatient surgical procedures with an expected length of stay greater
   than 23 hours require prior authorization at least 3 business days
   before the scheduled procedure date.
3. Durable medical equipment (DME) with a purchase or rental cost
   exceeding $500 requires prior authorization.
4. Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and chiropractic services
   beyond the initial 12 visits per calendar year require prior
   authorization to continue coverage.
5. Specialty pharmacy and infusion therapy administered in an outpatient
   facility require prior authorization regardless of cost.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION FOR PRIOR AUTHORIZATION SUBMISSION
- Physician order signed and dated by the ordering provider
- Clinical notes supporting medical necessity, including relevant
  diagnostic test results
- ICD-10 diagnosis code(s) supporting the medical necessity of the
  requested service
- CPT/HCPCS code(s) for the requested procedure or equipment
- For DME: a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN)

CLAIM DENIAL FOR MISSING PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Claims for services listed above submitted without a valid prior
authorization reference number will be denied with reason code
CO-197 (Precertification/Authorization Absent). Providers may submit a
retroactive authorization request within 180 days of the date of
service, accompanied by a written explanation of the extenuating
circumstances, but retroactive approval is not guaranteed.

APPEALS
A denial issued for lack of prior authorization may be appealed within
180 days of the denial notice. The appeal must include the missing
authorization documentation and a cover letter explaining why prior
authorization could not be obtained before the service was rendered.
