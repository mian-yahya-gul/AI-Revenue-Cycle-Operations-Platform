UnitedHealthcare Commercial Plan — Coverage Determination Guidelines
Policy Number: UHC-CD-2024-07
Effective Date: March 1, 2024

GENERAL COVERAGE PRINCIPLES
UnitedHealthcare covers services that are medically necessary, consistent
with generally accepted standards of medical practice, and not primarily
for the convenience of the patient or provider.

MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA
A service is considered medically necessary when documentation
establishes all of the following:
1. The service is appropriate for the symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment
   of the member's condition, illness, or injury.
2. The service is provided for the diagnosis or direct treatment of the
   condition, not for screening purposes unless explicitly listed as a
   covered preventive benefit.
3. The service is delivered in accordance with generally accepted
   standards of medical practice.
4. The service is not primarily for the convenience of the patient,
   physician, or other health care provider.
5. Clinical documentation supports the level of service billed.

EXCLUDED SERVICES
The following are generally excluded from coverage unless a specific
plan rider states otherwise:
- Cosmetic procedures not related to reconstructive necessity following
  injury or disease
- Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by the FDA for
  the specific diagnosis billed
- Services rendered without a valid physician order
- Duplicate services billed for the same date of service and same
  provider without documented medical justification

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS FOR CLAIM SUBMISSION
Every claim must include: patient demographic and eligibility
information, ICD-10 diagnosis code(s) that support medical necessity,
CPT/HCPCS procedure code(s) consistent with the documented service,
rendering provider NPI, and the date of service. Claims missing any of
the above elements will be returned or denied as incomplete
(reason code CO-16).
