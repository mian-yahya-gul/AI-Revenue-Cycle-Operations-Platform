Aetna Commercial and Medicare Advantage — Precertification Policy
Policy Number: AET-PC-2024-03
Effective Date: February 1, 2024

PRECERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
Aetna requires precertification (prior authorization) for the following
services across all commercial and Medicare Advantage plans:
1. All non-emergent inpatient admissions.
2. Advanced imaging procedures including MRI, CT, and nuclear cardiology
   studies performed in an outpatient setting.
3. Elective surgical procedures with a global surgery period of 90 days
   or more.
4. Home health services beyond the first 60 days following an inpatient
   discharge.
5. Genetic and molecular diagnostic testing panels.

REQUIRED SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION
Requests must include a completed precertification request form, the
ordering physician's clinical notes, relevant lab or imaging results,
and the specific ICD-10 and CPT codes for the requested service. Aetna
will not process a precertification request that lacks a signed
physician order.

CLAIMS SUBMITTED WITHOUT PRECERTIFICATION
Claims for services requiring precertification that are submitted
without an approved precertification number will be denied under
reason code CO-197. Providers have 60 calendar days from the date of
denial to submit a retrospective review request with clinical
justification for why precertification was not obtained in advance.

MODIFIER REQUIREMENTS
Claims billed with modifier -25 (significant, separately identifiable
E/M service) or modifier -59 (distinct procedural service) must include
supporting documentation in the medical record clearly justifying the
use of the modifier. Claims with these modifiers lacking supporting
documentation are subject to automatic review and possible denial.

APPEALS PROCESS
Members and providers may appeal a precertification-related denial
within 180 days. Appeals should be submitted in writing and include all
clinical documentation that supports medical necessity, along with a
detailed explanation addressing the specific denial reason cited.
