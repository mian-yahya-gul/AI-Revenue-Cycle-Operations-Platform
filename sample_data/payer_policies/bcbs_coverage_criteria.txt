Blue Cross Blue Shield — Coverage and Claims Adjudication Guidelines
Policy Number: BCBS-CA-2024-18
Effective Date: June 1, 2024

COVERAGE STANDARD
BCBS covers services that are medically necessary and consistent with
nationally recognized clinical guidelines. Coverage determinations are
based on the clinical documentation submitted with, or available to
support, the claim.

CLAIM COMPLETENESS REQUIREMENTS
A complete claim includes: patient and subscriber identification
information, rendering and billing provider NPI, date(s) of service,
place of service code, ICD-10 diagnosis code(s), CPT/HCPCS procedure
code(s), units of service, and billed charges. Claims missing any
required field are rejected prior to adjudication and are not counted
against timely filing limits if resubmitted within 30 days of
rejection.

DUPLICATE CLAIM HANDLING
BCBS's claims editing system flags any claim matching an existing paid
or pending claim on patient, date of service, procedure code, and
rendering provider as a potential duplicate. Duplicate claims are
denied under reason code CO-18 unless accompanied by an appropriate
modifier (-76, -77, -91) and documentation supporting a distinct,
medically necessary repeat service.

MODIFIER AND BUNDLING EDITS
Claims are subject to National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits.
Procedure code combinations that are bundled under NCCI and billed
without an appropriate modifier will have the component code denied as
included in the primary procedure.
